Her method, written down

A team of agents, from zero

Serves
One manager
Prep
Two weeksI
Skill
No technical background needed
  1. Start with one conductor and only talk to that one. Let it hand out the work.
  2. If you don't understand a technical choice, ask for the options and the reasons. Then decide.
  3. Don't add a rule as soon as you think of it. Talk it through with your conductor first.

Chef's note: “I'm sure people alone could never have done it.”I

“First I work out what the job actually needs me to do, where I sit in the company, and who I work with. For a project, I get as much material as I can: the background, the result they want, and what counts as done. Every day there's a morning brief and a short stand-up about blockers and problems. Every meeting must end with action items; otherwise it probably didn't need to happen.”Y
