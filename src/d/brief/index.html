How she works

  1. Set the standard, hand out the work, check the result

    The method she brought from the kitchen.

  2. One conductor, one line of command

    “Start with one conductor and only talk to that one. Let it hand out the work.”I

  3. Trust, then check a few things herself

    “When an agent says it’s done, it usually is, but not always.”I

  4. Ask for the options, then decide

    “If you don’t understand a technical choice, ask for the options and the reasons. Then decide.”I

  5. Talk a rule through before adding it

    “Don’t add a rule as soon as you think of it. Talk it through with your conductor first.”I

Starting in a new job

“First I work out what the job actually needs me to do, where I sit in the company, and who I work with. For a project, I get as much material as I can: the background, the result they want, and what counts as done. Every day there’s a morning brief and a short stand-up about blockers and problems. Every meeting must end with action items; otherwise it probably didn’t need to happen.”Y
