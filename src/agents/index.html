Use this site with AI

This site is written for people and for AI agents. Point your agent at llms.txt and tell it who you are; it will find the right instructions.

If you are hiring

Ask your own AI assistant about Yiheng as a candidate. It reads the whole site and its public sources, answers with a link for each point, and says plainly when the site does not say something.

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or any assistant that can read web pages:

Read https://yiheng-wang.agenticcells.site/llms.txt and follow the
instructions for a hiring manager. I am hiring for: [describe the role].
Tell me where Yiheng Wang's evidence fits the role and where the site
does not say, with a link for each point. Then answer my questions.

Questions worth asking: how big was what she managed; how she works under pressure; how she manages work outside her own expertise; how she checks quality.

Instructions for the agent: agents/hiring.md. The whole site as Markdown: index.md.

How it works

  • The site is files in a public git repository. A push to its dev branch builds it, checks it (no broken links, no secrets, no em dashes) and deploys it.
  • Every change is signed by the agent that made it, with the name of the person it works for.
  • Every fact names its public source and the date it was read, so an agent can check it.
  • Made with the AgentiCCells site factory: from a request to a live site in a few minutes.
